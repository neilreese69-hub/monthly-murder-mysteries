# CASE #001
# The Bellweather Murder

**Bellweather, Iowa — August 21, 2026**

## Your Assignment

At 9:38 p.m., Arthur Bell, owner of the historic Hawthorne House, was found dead inside his private office.

Five people were still inside the building.

All five had a reason to be angry with Arthur.

All five gave statements to investigators.

At least one statement contains a lie.

Your job is not to guess.

Your job is to determine:

**Who was responsible for Arthur Bell's death?**

Then prove your answer using the evidence.

---

## The Victim

**Arthur Bell, 62**

Arthur owned Hawthorne House, a historic hotel overlooking Bellweather's courthouse square.

He was well known in town, but not especially well liked.

During dinner that evening, Arthur announced that he intended to make "a significant change" concerning the hotel.

He refused to explain what that meant.

Twenty minutes later, he went to his private office.

He never came back.

---

## The Five People

### Margaret Bell
Arthur's sister.

She believed Arthur intended to sell the hotel, despite their family's history with the property.

### Daniel Cross
Arthur's business partner.

Arthur had recently questioned several unexplained business expenses.

### Evelyn Price
Bellweather's town historian.

Arthur had threatened to remove several historical documents from the hotel.

### Thomas Reed
Editor of the Bellweather Gazette.

Arthur had threatened legal action over an article Thomas had published.

### Laura Bennett
Manager of Hawthorne House.

Laura had worked at the hotel for nineteen years.

She had recently learned that Arthur was considering replacing her.

---

## The Evening

Dinner ended shortly after 9:00 p.m.

Arthur told the group he had one final matter to handle.

At approximately 9:05 p.m., he went toward his office.

From that point forward, the accounts begin to disagree.

Margaret says she spoke with Arthur at about 9:10.

Daniel says he went upstairs at approximately 9:15.

Evelyn says she left to make a telephone call at about 9:20.

Thomas says he went outside at approximately 9:25.

Laura says she remained downstairs the entire time.

At 9:38 p.m., Laura discovered Arthur in his office.

---

## The Office

The office had:

- One hallway entrance
- One window facing the courtyard
- A desk
- A wall clock
- A telephone
- Two chairs
- A bookshelf

The window was closed.

The door was unlocked.

There was no obvious sign that an outsider had entered the building.

On Arthur's desk investigators found a sheet of hotel stationery.

Written on it were three words:

**"Tomorrow. Eight o'clock."**

---

## Your Investigation

The remaining case materials contain:

- Suspect statements
- The official timeline
- Physical evidence
- Telephone records
- Documents from Hawthorne House
- Additional clues

Study everything carefully.

Some information will be important.

Some information will be misleading.

Your final accusation must explain the evidence.

**The solution is kept separately.**

Do not look for the solution until you have made your accusation.
