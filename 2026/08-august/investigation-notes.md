# CASE #001 — INVESTIGATION NOTES

## Additional Investigator Findings

The following information was discovered after the initial interviews.

Read carefully. Small details may matter.

---

## Finding 1 — Arthur's Appointment

Arthur's desk calendar contains an appointment for:

**Saturday — 8:00 a.m.**

The appointment is marked:

**L.B.**

Laura Bennett confirms that she uses those initials.

She says the appointment was an ordinary work meeting.

---

## Finding 2 — The Blue Folder

The blue folder found on the staircase contains Hawthorne House's weekly administrative schedule.

Laura is responsible for updating the schedule.

No other employee regularly carries the folder.

The folder contains the original schedule for Saturday morning.

---

## Finding 3 — The Downstairs Telephone

The hotel's landline was located beside the front desk.

The telephone record shows:

**Outgoing call — 9:17 p.m.**  
**Duration — 4 minutes**

The person who made the call has not been identified.

The front desk area was empty during part of this period.

---

## Finding 4 — Evelyn's Telephone Story

Evelyn said she used her mobile phone around 9:20 p.m.

Her phone contains no outgoing call during that period.

When investigators asked her about this, Evelyn admitted she had actually used the hotel's downstairs telephone.

She says she became embarrassed because the call was personal and did not want to explain it.

She refuses to identify the person she called.

---

## Finding 5 — The Hallway Clock

The hallway clock was six minutes fast.

Therefore, when the clock displayed:

**9:30**

the actual time was:

**9:24**

This matters when comparing statements.

---

## Finding 6 — The Office Clock

The clock inside Arthur's office was eleven minutes slow.

Therefore, when it displayed:

**9:30**

the actual time was:

**9:41**

The clock was still running when investigators arrived.

---

## Finding 7 — The Staircase Witness

The witness who reported seeing the blue folder could not identify the person's face.

However, the witness remembered one additional detail.

The person carrying the folder was wearing a **dark green cardigan**.

Investigators later learned that Laura had been wearing a dark green cardigan during dinner.

---

## Finding 8 — Laura's Clothing

Laura was still wearing the same dark green cardigan when investigators interviewed her.

She says she never went upstairs after dinner.

---

## Finding 9 — The Administrative Folder

A page inside the blue folder has a handwritten note:

**"See Arthur before 8."**

The handwriting has not yet been conclusively identified.

The note appears to refer to the Saturday morning appointment.

---

# DETECTIVE'S CHALLENGE

You now have several independent pieces of information.

Try to connect them.

Ask yourself:

### Who had access to the blue folder?

### Who was seen carrying it?

### Who says they never went upstairs?

### Why would the folder matter?

### What does the 8:00 a.m. appointment tell you?

### Which statements are lies?

And most importantly:

**Which lie can be connected to the physical evidence?**
