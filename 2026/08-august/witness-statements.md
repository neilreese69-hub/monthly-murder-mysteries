# CASE #001 — WITNESS STATEMENTS

## Statement: Margaret Bell

**Time interviewed:** 11:15 p.m.

> "Arthur and I argued earlier. I won't pretend otherwise. Around 9:10, I went to his office because I wanted him to reconsider selling the hotel."

Margaret says Arthur was alive and speaking normally when she left.

> "He told me he had already made up his mind."

Margaret says she returned to the dining room afterward.

She says she did not see anyone enter the office.

---

## Statement: Daniel Cross

**Time interviewed:** 11:25 p.m.

> "I went upstairs around 9:15. I had left my coat somewhere and thought I might have taken it upstairs."

Daniel says he searched briefly.

> "I didn't find it, so I came back down."

He says he did not see anyone on the staircase.

When asked whether he entered Arthur's office, Daniel says:

> "No."

---

## Statement: Evelyn Price

**Time interviewed:** 11:40 p.m.

> "I left the dining room around 9:20 because I needed to make a telephone call."

Evelyn says she used her mobile phone.

> "It was a personal call. I don't remember exactly how long it lasted."

She says she returned downstairs approximately ten minutes later.

Evelyn admits she and Arthur had argued about the historical documents earlier that evening.

---

## Statement: Thomas Reed

**Time interviewed:** 11:55 p.m.

> "I went outside around 9:25. I was expecting a taxi."

Thomas says he remained near the front entrance.

> "I didn't go anywhere near Arthur's office."

When asked when the taxi arrived, Thomas initially says:

> "Around 9:30, maybe a little before."

Investigators later obtained the taxi company's arrival record.

---

## Statement: Laura Bennett

**Time interviewed:** 12:10 a.m.

Laura appears calm during the interview.

> "After dinner, I stayed downstairs. I was preparing the dining room for breakfast."

Laura says she never went upstairs.

> "I didn't have any reason to."

When asked whether she spoke with Arthur after he left the dining room, Laura says:

> "No."

Laura confirms that she had access to Hawthorne House's internal schedule book.

She says that access was part of her normal job.

---

# INVESTIGATOR'S NOTE

The statements contain several claims that can be checked against other evidence.

Do not decide who is responsible based on a single statement.

Compare each statement with:

- The timeline
- Telephone records
- Building records
- Physical evidence
- Other witnesses

A contradiction may be important.

It may also be a deliberate distraction.

**The evidence file must be examined before making an accusation.**
