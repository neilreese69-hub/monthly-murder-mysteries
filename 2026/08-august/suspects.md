# CASE #001 — SUSPECT DOSSIERS

## 1. Margaret Bell

**Age:** 59  
**Relationship to Arthur:** Younger sister

Margaret and Arthur inherited their family's connection to Hawthorne House after their parents died.

Margaret believes Arthur has forgotten what the property means to the family.

### Possible motive

Margaret believes Arthur is preparing to sell Hawthorne House.

If that happens, Margaret could lose the property and everything she associates with her family's history.

### What investigators know

Margaret argued with Arthur earlier in the evening.

She admits being angry with him.

She denies threatening him.

---

## 2. Daniel Cross

**Age:** 47  
**Relationship to Arthur:** Business partner

Daniel helped Arthur manage the financial side of Hawthorne House.

Arthur had recently begun questioning several unexplained business expenses.

### Possible motive

If Arthur discovered serious financial problems, Daniel's business reputation could be damaged.

Daniel insists the accounting problems were ordinary bookkeeping mistakes.

### What investigators know

Daniel says he went upstairs during the critical period.

He claims he was looking for his coat.

His coat was later found downstairs.

---

## 3. Evelyn Price

**Age:** 55  
**Relationship to Arthur:** Town historian

Evelyn has spent years documenting Bellweather's history.

Several historically important documents were stored at Hawthorne House.

### Possible motive

Arthur planned to remove some of the documents from the hotel.

Evelyn strongly opposed the idea.

### What investigators know

Evelyn admits she was upset with Arthur.

She says she left the dining area to make a telephone call.

The telephone records will become important.

---

## 4. Thomas Reed

**Age:** 42  
**Relationship to Arthur:** Newspaper editor

Thomas is the editor of the Bellweather Gazette.

He recently published an article criticizing Arthur's management of Hawthorne House.

### Possible motive

Arthur had threatened to sue Thomas and the newspaper.

Thomas says he was not worried about the threat.

### What investigators know

Thomas says he went outside to wait for a taxi.

His taxi did not arrive when he originally claimed it did.

---

## 5. Laura Bennett

**Age:** 51  
**Relationship to Arthur:** Hawthorne House manager

Laura has worked at Hawthorne House for nineteen years.

She knows the building, its routines, and its staff better than anyone.

### Possible motive

Laura recently learned that Arthur was considering replacing her.

Losing the job would mean losing the position she had held for almost two decades.

### What investigators know

Laura says she remained downstairs throughout the critical period.

She says she never went upstairs.

She also had access to Hawthorne House's internal schedule and administrative records.

---

# DETECTIVE'S WARNING

A motive does not prove guilt.

An alibi does not automatically prove innocence.

A lie does not automatically mean someone is the murderer.

Your job is to determine which pieces of information actually connect together.

Look for:

- Contradictions
- Impossible times
- Information only one person could know
- Evidence that confirms or destroys an alibi
- Details that appear unimportant at first

**The murderer is not identified in this document.**
